# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Project Name
MSME AI Operations Copilot

## 1. Business Problem
Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) make up the backbone of the economy, yet they frequently operate with limited resources and minimal technical tools. Many local shop owners, retail managers, and small business operators track their inventory and sales manually or using basic spreadsheets (like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets).

As a result, they face:
- **Manual Inventory Tracking:** Difficulty in keeping track of real-time stock levels, leading to stockouts or overstocking.
- **Hidden Trends:** Inability to spot sales patterns, seasonal trends, and slow-moving items.
- **Supplier Delays & Inefficiencies:** Overlooking delays in restocking due to supplier lead times.
- **Lack of Affordable Intelligence:** Professional Business Intelligence (BI) tools are expensive, complex, and require specialized data analysts to operate.

## 2. Business Goal
The goal of the **MSME AI Operations Copilot** is to democratize business intelligence by providing an easy-to-use, AI-driven assistant that acts as a virtual operations manager. By simply uploading their operational spreadsheets, business owners should instantly receive inventory analysis, financial highlights, alerts for critical issues, and actionable operations recommendations.

## 3. Target Users
- **Small Shop Owners:** Grocery stores, local retail outlets, small apparel shops.
- **MSME Managers:** Operations supervisors who manage ordering and stock control.
- **Local Businesses:** Family-owned distributors or service businesses with physical products.

## 4. Expected Outcome
A simple web application where users upload their standard sales and inventory spreadsheets and receive:
- **Inventory Insights:** Categorized list of fast-moving, overstocked, and low-stock products.
- **Sales Analysis:** Aggregated metrics for total revenue, product profit margins, and performance tracking.
- **AI Business Recommendations:** Actionable advice on ordering schedules, restocking, and sales improvement from an intelligent operations virtual manager.
- **Operations Report:** A summary report that can be exported or printed for business planning.
